I grew up dubbing cassettes and eventually burning CDs with a selection of tunes I wanna hear, in the order I wanna hear them.

That's what led me into DJing around 2001. It's been a really fulfilling journey of evolving that passion and following it into so much more.

Between playing live sets, it can be very fulfilling to return to those roots and put together studio mixes with the intention of simply crafting a mood or story for myself to come back and listen to.

This one is definitely among them. I was pleased that other people seemed to enjoy it as well. If you do, I'd love to hear from you!
